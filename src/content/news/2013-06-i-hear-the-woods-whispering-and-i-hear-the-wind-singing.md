[I Hear the Woods Whispering](/discography/i-hear-the-woods-whispering/) (four tracks, 72 minutes) and [I Hear the Wind Singing](/discography/i-hear-the-wind-singing/) (five tracks, 56 minutes) are two new albums by Oöphoi, released on Practising Nature, a division of Databloem. Both albums contain previously unreleased music.

Databloem explained how they came about. Not long before his passing, Oöphoi gave the label the recordings of two previously unreleased parts of the “I Hear” trilogy. Part one, [I Hear the Water Dreaming](/discography/i-hear-the-water-dreaming/), was a collaboration with [Louisa John-Krol](https://www.louisajohn-krol.com/), released in 2005. The other two parts were first meant as further collaborations, but for some reason the plan never got off the ground. At Gigi’s request, Databloem edited and released the basic tracks, with artwork by his beloved wife, Alessandra. In retrospect, both albums showcase Oöphoi’s talent for creating aural paintings of nature in a pure and soothing way.
