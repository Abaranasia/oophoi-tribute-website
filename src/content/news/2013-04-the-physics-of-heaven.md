[The Physics of Heaven](/discography/the-physics-of-heaven/) is a collaboration between the Italian ambient master Oöphoi (Gianluigi Gasparetti) and the Spanish ambient composer [Ran Kirlian](https://www.rankirlian.com/). It is a limited-edition album released on the [Altera Orbe](https://alteraorbe.es/) label on CD-R, FLAC and MP3.

Ran Kirlian on the album:

> The Physics of Heaven was an unfinished project that my friend Gigi asked me to finish, adding “anything I wanted to add without restrictions”. So I added layers of pads, choirs, voices, field recordings and more, while trying to stay faithful to the original concept of the work: a journey, a transition between life and death. In the end, it is a collection of feelings and ideas that explores the meaning of life and the meaning of death through different spiritual traditions and mythologies.

As their mutual friend Dave Ebacher wrote in the introduction to the album:

> On “Amnios: The Complete Recordings”, Oöphoi envisioned the very beginnings of life. Now in collaboration with Ran Kirlian, Oöphoi explores what Heaven may be like on “The Physics of Heaven”. From the first Breath and Ascend, the ethereal chords and pads are light-filled, providing the listener with a broad sense of uneasy serenity with natural sounds. Seamlessly, a sagacity of the enigmatic prevails as one looks across the White Shores of fluid pads with no end in sight... Just past the midway point, the colors turn to more obscure hues through the granular drones of Transmigration, unfolding only to The Golden Fields of peace with some of the brightest tenors over waves that fade, leaving the last few notes of life. Upon death, there is a reflection on legacy, the footsteps left behind...
>
> Oöphoi and Ran Kirlian have left us with not just the soundscapes to contemplate, but the eternal.
